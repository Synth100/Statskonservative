About NKP

A party website built like a real public website, not a local demo file.

The purpose of this project is to give NKP a proper public-facing website: branded visuals, a responsive layout, a clear programme structure, and a signup flow that actually stores submissions in a hosted database.

The design below is intentionally simple to maintain. You can later expand it with extra pages such as a party report, manifesto transcript, policy PDFs, press releases, or a members-only area.

Core priorities

The platform at a glance

Family and responsibility

Strong families, stable communities, and a social order built on duty, care, and long-term cohesion.

Christian values

A public culture that respects Christianity as a foundational source of moral language, restraint, dignity, and justice.

Welfare with obligations

A strong welfare state, but one tied to work, contribution, and reciprocal social responsibility.

Borders and integration

Controlled migration, real integration requirements, and a state capable of enforcing its laws and borders.

Programme

Detailed policy themes

Europe of nations

Cooperation between states is acceptable, but power should remain rooted as closely as possible in national democratic communities.

Stewardship and realism

Nature should be protected, but environmental policy must not become a tool for social decline, economic self-harm, or symbolic politics.

Institutions that hold society together

Parliament, law, schools, and civic institutions should reinforce continuity, responsibility, and national coherence.

Culture, continuity, identity

National culture is not a private hobby. It is part of the shared inheritance a society has a right to defend and pass on.

“A strong society is not only prosperous. It is morally coherent, socially responsible, culturally rooted, and capable of governing itself.”
